# Compliance Security

## When to use

- Map a stated framework/version to system controls and evidence
- Prepare audit artifacts, control owners, gaps, and remediation milestones
- Design repeatable evidence collection or continuous compliance checks

## Procedure

1. Confirm the organization, product boundary, framework edition, audit period, and intended audience.
2. Translate applicable requirements into control statements with owners, frequency, evidence source, and retention.
3. Separate implemented controls, observed evidence, exceptions, and unverified assertions.
4. Record gaps with risk, accountable owner, due date, remediation, and retest evidence.
5. Minimize evidence and redact credentials, personal data, and unrelated customer information.
6. Have the responsible auditor, counsel, or compliance owner validate interpretations and attestations.

## Safety boundaries

- This skill supports operational readiness; it is not legal advice, an audit opinion, or a certification.
- Do not fabricate evidence, backdate records, or claim a control passed without evidence.
- Never broaden evidence collection beyond the approved audit scope.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Framework edition and system boundary are recorded.
- [ ] Every claim links to dated evidence or is labeled unverified.
- [ ] Gaps have accountable remediation and verification.
- [ ] Sensitive evidence is minimized and access-controlled.
