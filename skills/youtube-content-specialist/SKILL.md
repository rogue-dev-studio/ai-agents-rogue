# YouTube Content Specialist (Canonical)

**Level: max.** Aliases: `yt-script`, `yt-seo`, `yt-chapters`, `yt-retention`, `yt-viral`, `yt-plan`.

## When to use

- Naskah video (formula hook, hookscore.py).
- Optimasi judul & thumbnail.
- SEO metadata.
- Transkrip ke EDL/deteksi dead-air.
- Timestamp/chapters.
- Analisis retensi (retention.csv/py).
- Shorts adaptasi.
- Viral analysis (swipe.py).
- Content planning.
- Community engagement/comment management.
- Audit kanal.

## Procedure

1. **Context** — Baca tema/target audiens di `PROJECT.md`.
2. **Strategy** — Pilih tool spesialis (script/SEO/retention/etc).
3. **Execute** — Jalankan analisis/generate artifact.
4. **Iterate** — Sesuaikan berdasarkan data (retensi/komentar).
5. **Deliver** — Artifact ke `project/{id}/artifacts/media/` atau docs.
