# Anti-Slop Visual (antislop-visual)

Prevent the "AI-look" in images and videos. Ensure visual outputs are organic, textural, and avoid the uncanny perfection of generic AI generation.

## Objective
Eliminate "Visual Slop"—the over-saturated, plastic, and hyper-symmetrical patterns that immediately signal an AI-generated image.

## Visual Guardrails

### 1. Texture & Surface
- **Ban Plastic Skin**: Stop generating poreless, perfectly smooth skin. Demand "skin pores", "natural imperfections", "fine wrinkles", and "realistic texture".
- **Ban Over-Saturation**: Avoid the "neon-glow" or "hyper-vibrant" look. Prefer "natural lighting", "muted tones", or "film-grade color grading".
- **Organic Imperfections**: Push for "lens flare", "film grain", "dust", or "slight motion blur" to break the digital perfection.

### 2. Composition & Lighting
- **Avoid Center-Symmetry**: Ban the "perfectly centered" subject. Use "rule of thirds", "dynamic angles", or "candid framing".
- **Natural Light**: Stop using "cinematic lighting" as a generic prompt. Specify "golden hour", "overcast soft light", "harsh midday sun", or "single source lamp".
- **Depth of Field**: Avoid the "everything-is-blurred-except-one-pixel" look. Use realistic "f/2.8" or "f/8" depth descriptors.

### 3. Video & Motion (if applicable)
- **Ban Morphing**: Prevent the "liquid-like" transitions between frames. Demand "consistent geometry" and "physical weight".
- **Human Motion**: Avoid the "slow-motion float". Push for "natural gait", "micro-expressions", and "erratic human movements".

## Quality Gate
Visual is "Slop" if:
- The skin looks like polished plastic or wax.
- The colors look like a generic "digital art" filter.
- The composition is too perfectly centered and symmetrical.
- The lighting is "magical" without a physical source.
