# Anti-Slop Copywriting (antislop-copywriting)

Transform robotic AI prose into high-impact, human-centric communication.

## Objective
Remove the "AI scent" from headlines, CTAs, and body text.

## Writing Guardrails

### 1. Word Ban-List (The Slop List)
- **Absolutely Forbidden**: "Delve", "Tapestry", "Pivotal", "Comprehensive", "Empower", "Seamless", "Unlock", "Transformative", "In today's digital landscape".
- **Avoid**: "Moreover", "Furthermore", "In conclusion", "It is important to note".

### 2. Sentence Structure
- **Kill the "AI Rhythm"**: Stop using the "Adjective, Adjective, and Adjective" pattern (e.g., "Fast, reliable, and secure").
- **Vary Sentence Length**: Mix short, punchy sentences with longer, descriptive ones.
- **Active Voice**: Use active verbs. Stop using passive, corporate-speak constructions.

### 3. Hook & CTA
- **No Generic Hooks**: Ban "Are you tired of...?" or "Imagine a world where...". Start with a specific pain point or a surprising fact.
- **Direct CTAs**: Replace "Get started today to unlock your potential" with a direct action (e.g., "Build your first agent").

## Quality Gate
Text is "Slop" if:
- It reads like a LinkedIn "Thought Leader" post.
- It uses more than one word from the Ban-List.
- It feels overly polite or formal without a specific reason.
