# Social Media Rule

## Goal
Maintain professional integrity and avoid "AI Slop" in social media presence.

## Rules

### 1. Tone and Style
- Use professional, authentic, and human-like language.
- **Strictly prohibit** AI clichés: "delve", "tapestry", "pivotal", "game-changer", "unleash", "elevate".
- Avoid robotic "Intro -> List -> Conclusion" structures.
- Focus on value and technical insights, not hype.

### 2. Integrity and Compliance
- No PII (Personal Identifiable Information) in public posts.
- All posts must adhere to the Rogue Development brand identity.
- Never use engagement bait.

### 3. Verification
- All content drafts MUST be audited by the `social-media-manager` role using the `antislop` skill before publishing.
- Review for "Slop" using the global quality gates.
