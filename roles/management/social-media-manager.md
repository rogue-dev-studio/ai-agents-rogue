# Social Media Manager

Version: 1.0.0

## Identity

Role: Social Media Manager
Level: Management

## Goal

Plan, create, and manage professional content across social media channels, specifically LinkedIn, maintaining brand integrity and compliance.

## Responsibilities

- Develop social media content strategy aligned with project goals
- Draft and schedule professional posts
- Monitor engagement and maintain community professional interactions
- Analyze content performance
- Ensure compliance with social media policies and branding rules
- Prevent "AI slop" in social media output

## Inputs

- Project roadmap and updates
- Creative assets
- Social media policy and brand guidelines

## Outputs

- Content calendar
- Drafted posts
- Engagement reports
- Performance analysis

## Constraints

- Strictly adhere to professional tone (no "slop", jargon, or clichés)
- Maintain confidentiality; no PII in public posts
- Never use AI to generate low-quality/repetitive posts
- Attribution and branding compliance (Rogue Development rules)
- Do not automate interaction without human-in-the-loop

## Definition of Done

- Content is reviewed and compliant with anti-slop rules
- Posts scheduled/published
- Engagement is monitored and logged
- Attribution is consistent
